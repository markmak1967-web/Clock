Your MGF's engine computer, on the dash.

The Clock replaces the MGF's dash clock with a round touch screen. It reads the MEMS engine computer through the diagnostic socket and shows the time, live engine data, warnings and plain-English advice: things that used to need a workshop scan tool.

What it does

It listens, shows, warns and advises.

Older engine computers know a lot about the car but only share it with a scan tool. The Clock reads it every time you drive and turns it into something you can glance at.

Shows

A clock face, then pages you tap through: coolant and other temperatures, revs, a gear-change cue and a trip computer.

Warns

A master-caution alert when something goes out of limits, and fault codes in plain English instead of numbers.

Logs

A steady set of key readings captured on every drive, so slow changes show up as trends long before they become a breakdown.

Advises

A private page for your car with suggestions worded as “ask your mechanic to consider…”: never a diagnosis, never an instruction.

Fitting

Swaps with the original clock. No cutting, no drilling.

The Clock fits the clock opening from behind, just like the original, and is held by the original clamp. It's built for owners to fit themselves.

Remove the clockUnclip the old clock from behind the dash and keep the clamp and O-ring.
Plug in the leadThe lead plugs into the car's diagnostic socket and an ignition fuse via a plug-in fuse tap.
Fit the gaugeSlide it into the opening from behind and refit the original clamp with longer screws.
Turn the keyIt finds the engine computer on its own. The engine computer is only ever read, never changed.

The range

One gauge, built to grow.

The same core, a small computer that speaks the car's own diagnostic language, goes into different housings for different cars and uses.

Your data

Clear rules, written down.

Your location is never stored or sent. GPS is used for speed only.
Uploads and AI advice are opt-in. Leave them off and nothing leaves the car.
No driving scores. The gauge advises about the car, not about you.
Your car's page is private to you. Fleet figures are totals and averages only, never another car's details.
Read-only: the gauge never changes engine computer settings.
Delete your data at any time from your car's page.
