Capability Statement

The Clock – for MGF

Independent development project
Townsville, Queensland, Australia
Status: alpha testing · November 2026

1Summary

The Clock is a family of driver instruments that read pre-2000 engine computers through their diagnostic link and present live data, warnings and maintenance guidance in the car. That information has otherwise only been available to workshop scan tools. The first product replaces the MGF dash clock and reads the Rover MEMS 1.9 engine management system.

2Core capabilities

Engine computer communication

  • Reads the Rover MEMS 1.9 engine computer over the K-line diagnostic link.
  • Self-recovering connection, built for an installed unit that is not easily accessible.
  • Read-only: does not change engine computer settings. Limited actuator tests only, excluding injectors, ignition coils and settings.
  • Detects and reports Rover MEMS 2J (support in progress).

Driver display

  • Clock face as the rest screen; tap-through pages for temperatures, engine speed, gear-change cue and trip computer.
  • Master-caution alerting on out-of-limit readings.
  • Fault codes translated into plain English.

Standard interfaces

  • OBD-II output over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (ELM327 convention), so existing OBD apps can read the car.
  • Settings and data page served by the gauge over its own Wi-Fi.

Logging and trend capture

  • Aviation-style recording: a stable set of key metrics captured on every drive cycle (start, mid and end temperatures, cycles, engine hours, out-of-limit flags).
  • Motion sensor data (acceleration, sway, roll) and GPS-derived speed and distance.

Owner and fleet analysis

  • Opt-in upload of drive summaries to a hosted data store.
  • Private owner page per car: history, open advice, and the car's position within fleet ranges.
  • Fleet statistics and a periodic “State of the Fleet” summary for opted-in owners.
  • Optional AI-assisted advice, always worded “ask your mechanic to consider…”, never as a diagnosis or instruction.

Fleet administration

  • Admin dashboard: owners, gauges, configuration, per-measurement log statistics.
  • Fleet firmware update workflow (planned).

6Data handling

  • Vehicle location is never stored or transmitted. GPS is used for speed and distance only.
  • Uploads and AI advice are opt-in. With them switched off, no data leaves the car.
  • No driving scores. Advice concerns the condition of the car, not the driver.
  • Owner pages are private to one car. Fleet outputs show totals and averages only, never another car's details.
  • Owners can delete their data at any time from their car's page.
